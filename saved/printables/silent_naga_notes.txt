Notes
- Separate-pin hinges: print the bodies and the pin parts on their own, line up the knuckles and push the
  pin in from the end without a head (print pins standing up, head down, for a round shaft).
- The gap is the designed clearance between moving surfaces. Below about 0.3 mm most FDM printers
  fuse the parts; raise it in the hinge settings if the hinge prints solid.
